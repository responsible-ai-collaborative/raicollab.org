# About

The Responsible AI Collaborative is a non-profit organization bringing together multiple contributors to produce a safer world with AI. Its only project is the [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/), which is the leading catalgue of harm events (i.e., "incidents") in the real world.

Most information about TheCollaborative can be found at the [AIID website,](https://incidentdatabase.ai/) including the current list of [collaborators](https://incidentdatabase.ai/about/#collaborators) jointly operating for safety.
